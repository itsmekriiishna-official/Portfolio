My story

I grew up in Kuala Lumpur and thought working hard would guarantee a particular outcome. Missing out on a scholarship was an early lesson that it doesn't always work that way.

I started studying law in 2015, then left after three months. Over the next few years I tried several jobs, from customer service and retail to real estate, advertising, and digital marketing.

A detour into forex ended with me losing about $5,000 to a scam. It taught me to be more careful about chasing shortcuts.

After another job that didn't feel right, I decided to try design.

At a 24-hour hackathon in 2019, I joined two strangers, and our team placed in the top ten. I left with my first UI/UX job offer and soon joined Fave as a product designer.

During the pandemic I started making YouTube videos and learned Final Cut Pro as I went. The channel reached 10,000 subscribers a few months later; I left my job in 2022.

Today I'm Video Lead at Mobbin. The channel grew from 200 to more than 10,000 subscribers in two months.

A visit to San Francisco inspired me to host demo nights with my friend Joe at sidequest.camp, bringing people together through creative work.
