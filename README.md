# 💫 About Me:
As a passionate UI/UX Designer, I aim to craft user-centered, intuitive, Data Driven, Innovative and impactful digital experiences. With hands-on skills in wireframing, prototyping, and usability testing, I bring creativity and problem-solving into every project. I am eager to contribute fresh ideas, learn fast, and deliver designs that balance user delight with business value. <br>--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------<br>I Know Tools Using<br>I am most comfortable with Figma (2+ years) for UI design, prototyping, design systems, and collaboration.<br>I regularly use FigJam (2 years) for brainstorming, user flows, journey mapping, and workshops.<br>For UX planning and structure, I use Miro (1+ year) for flow mapping and information architecture, and Balsamiq (6+ months) for quick low-fidelity wireframes.<br>I have hands-on experience with Framer (1+ year) for interactive prototypes and motion-based UI exploration.<br>I have worked with Adobe XD (1 year) for UI design and prototyping in earlier projects.<br>I actively explore and adopt new and trending tools such as Figma Make, Google Stitch, and AI-assisted design tools to stay updated with modern workflows.<br>
